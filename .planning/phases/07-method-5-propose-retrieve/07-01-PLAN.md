<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: FastSAMInferencer with the output decoding written out</name>
  <files>src/object_search/inference/fastsam.py, tests/test_fastsam.py</files>
  <action>
`FastSAMInferencer(ONNXInferencer)`. Docstring states the input contract (1024² letterbox, the
fill value, RGB, scale 1/255) and writes the **full YOLOv8-seg decoding** explicitly:
  transpose `output0` → `[21504, 37]`; split into 4 box + 1 conf + 32 mask coeffs; filter by
  confidence; NMS on boxes; for survivors `masks = sigmoid(coeffs @ protos.reshape(32, -1))`
  reshaped to 256×256; **crop each mask to its own box** (mandatory — without it a mask bleeds
  outside its detection); upsample each to image resolution; undo the letterbox to get
  image-space boxes.

`predict(image) -> list[Proposal]` where `Proposal` (frozen) carries `box: BBox`,
`mask: NDArray | None`, `objectness: float`.

Tests: feed **synthetic** `output0`/`output1` tensors of the verified shapes and assert the
decoding produces the expected boxes/masks (model-free, runs in CI). Assert the crop-to-box step
actually zeroes mask pixels outside the box. Skip any real-weight test when the file is absent.
  </action>
