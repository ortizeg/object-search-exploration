# Demo Asset Licences and Provenance (DOC-01)

Every file committed under `assets/demo/` is accounted for here, plus the licences of the
model weights that `pixi run fetch-models` downloads (those weights are **gitignored** and
never live in the repo, but their terms constrain how this repo may be shared, so they are
recorded here too).

Two of the model licences are real constraints on publishing or network-exposing this repo;
they are called out in the **Model weights** section and dated. Milestone 1 is a private,
local, single-user exploration, under which none of those obligations trigger.

Three frames copied from the sibling project `basketball-2d-to-3d` and downscaled to a 1600 px
long edge (JPEG q92, ≈ 340 KB each) so the repo stays small. They contain multiple players —

**Licence / provenance — recorded honestly.** These are frames decoded from a basketball
broadcast video used as test footage in the sibling project. The underlying broadcast content
is **copyrighted by its original rights holder**; it is **not** under a permissive licence.
They are included here solely for **local, non-commercial research and demonstration** — the
same private-use posture under which the SuperPoint and FastSAM weights are acceptable.

> ⚠️ **Revisit before publishing or network-exposing this repo.** These frames are **not
> redistributable**. If this repository is ever made public, replace them with
> permissively-licensed or self-captured footage, or remove them.

## Model weights (fetched by `pixi run fetch-models`, gitignored — never committed)

Recorded here because their terms bind the repository even though the files are not in it.
See `.planning/research/MODELS.md` for the full runtime-verified analysis.

| Model | Registry key | Licence | Constraint |
|-------|--------------|---------|------------|
| DINOv2-small (ONNX) | `dinov2-small` | **Apache-2.0** | Clean. Inherited from `facebook/dinov2-small`; the `onnx-community` derivative declares no licence field, so the inheritance is recorded explicitly. Verdict **Adopt** recorded in `docs/library-reviews/dinov2.md`. |
| SuperPoint (ONNX) | `superpoint` | **MagicLeap non-commercial research-only** | The weights are non-commercial; the derivatives clause covers the ONNX file, so it must **never be redistributed**. Satisfied at zero cost because weights are gitignored (INFRA-11). |
| FastSAM-s (ONNX) | `fastsam-s` | **AGPL-3.0** | The exported `.onnx` embeds the AGPL licence string. Private local use triggers nothing; **publishing this repo or network-exposing the API fires AGPL §13**. Revisit before either. |

> ⚠️ **Two dated triggers to revisit before this repo is published or network-exposed:**
> (1) **SuperPoint** weights are non-commercial and non-redistributable; (2) **FastSAM** is
> AGPL-3.0 and network exposure invokes §13. Both are acceptable under the Milestone 1 scope
> (local, single-user, private). The MIT/Apache-licensed **MobileSAM** is the documented clean
> alternative to FastSAM, at the cost of a hand-ported automatic-mask pipeline.
